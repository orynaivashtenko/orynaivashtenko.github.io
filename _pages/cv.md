**Oryna Ivashtenko**
Department of Particle Physics and Astrophysics, Weizmann Institute of Science, Rehovot, Israel
[oryna.ivashtenko@weizmann.ac.il](mailto:oryna.ivashtenko@weizmann.ac.il)

**Research interests.** Statistically calibrated detection, parameter estimation, and population inference for weak astrophysical signals in complex noise, with applications to exoplanets, stars, and compact objects.
